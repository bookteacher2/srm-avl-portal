24X SOLUTION GROUP (THAILAND) CO., LTD.
SUPPLIER CODE OF CONDUCT
Version 1.0

By registering on the 24X Supplier Relationship Management (SRM) Portal and
seeking inclusion on the Approved Vendor List (AVL), every supplier agrees to
uphold this Code of Conduct.

1. ETHICS & INTEGRITY
   - No bribery, corruption, facilitation payments, fraud or collusion.
   - Declare any conflict of interest with 24X staff or affiliates.
   - Compete fairly and honestly in all sourcing and bidding activities.

2. HEALTH, SAFETY & ENVIRONMENT (HSE)
   - Comply with all applicable HSE laws and 24X site requirements.
   - Maintain valid safety certifications and insurance.
   - Report incidents promptly and work to zero-harm principles.

3. QUALITY
   - Deliver goods and services to agreed specifications and standards.
   - Maintain quality management appropriate to the scope of supply.

4. LEGAL & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
   - Comply with labour, tax, licensing and anti-money-laundering laws.
   - Respect human rights; prohibit child and forced labour.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY & DATA PROTECTION
   - Protect 24X confidential information.
   - Handle personal data in line with Thailand's PDPA.

6. ACCURACY OF INFORMATION
   - All information and documents submitted are true, current and complete.
   - Notify 24X of material changes (ownership, licences, insurance, etc.).

BREACH
   Breach of this Code may result in suspension or removal from the AVL and,
   in serious cases, blacklisting and legal action.
